# 🧠 NotifAi News

**NotifAi News** is an AI-powered news aggregator built to summarize global stories, spark unique debates, and deliver balanced multi-perspective insights — now partially on-chain with **BNB Smart Chain (BSC)** integration.

---

## 🌍 Project Overview

NotifAi News fetches real-time stories from trusted global feeds and uses advanced AI models (OpenAI) to:
- Summarize each article in concise, neutral language.
- Present **three AI personalities** for every news story:
  - **Jessica Rebella** – A socialist voice representing the left-wing perspective.  
  - **John Davis** – A strong right-wing, pro-conservative opinion holder.  
  - **Joe Musk** – A conspiracy thinker who connects the dots between global events and hidden agendas.

This creates a modern digital forum that promotes **healthy debate** and **multi-angle storytelling** — all generated in real time by AI.

---

## 💠 BNB Smart Chain (BSC) Integration

NotifAi News is now partially connected to the **BNB Smart Chain** through an on-chain donation contract, allowing supporters to contribute directly via **BNB**.

### 🔗 Contract Details
- **Network:** BNB Smart Chain (BSC Mainnet)  
- **Token Used:** BNB  
- **Contract Address:** [`0x6a98b87f8116678ed98f74ae9a638bf30ebf3846`](https://bscscan.com/address/0x6a98b87f8116678ed98f74ae9a638bf30ebf3846)
- **Purpose:** Accept community donations in BNB to support the development and scaling of NotifAi’s AI infrastructure, mobile app, and blockchain experiments.

All donations are recorded transparently on-chain, contributing to our vision of making **news and AI accountability verifiable through decentralized networks.**

---

## 🪙 How to Donate (BNB)

1. Connect your **MetaMask** or any Web3 wallet to the **BNB Smart Chain**.  
2. Visit [NotifAi News](https://www.notifai.news).  
3. Click the **“Donate”** button on the top right or footer.  
4. Confirm the transaction — your donation will be recorded to the smart contract.

**Contract Source:** `NotifAiDonations.sol`
