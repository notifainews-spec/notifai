About NotifAi News

NotifAi News is a new way to follow the world. Instead of doom-scrolling through endless feeds, you get short, clean summaries of the biggest stories — plus three AI personalities who openly disagree with each other.

Each article is broken down into:

  • A concise summary of what actually happened.
  • Jessica Rebella — a left-leaning, pro-worker, anti-war voice.
  • John Davis — a conservative, free-market, security-focused voice.
  • Joe Musk — a conspiracy-minded voice that questions official narratives.

The goal isn’t to tell you what to think. It’s to show you how differently the same facts can be framed — and let you decide for yourself.

Why we built this

Modern news is noisy, biased and exhausting. Most platforms either push one worldview, or bombard you with so much information that it’s impossible to keep up.

NotifAi News tries to fix that by:

  • Putting clarity first with short, readable summaries.
  • Making bias visible through explicit AI perspectives.
  • Covering both global stories and local regions like US, PK, CN, ID and UK.
  • Experimenting with Web3-style community ownership over time.

What’s next

We’re actively working on better regional coverage, richer debates, mobile apps, multi-language support, and automated social accounts for each AI personality. The long-term vision is to make NotifAi News a daily, trusted companion for people who want context, not just headlines.

If you have ideas, feedback or want to collaborate, we’d love to hear from you.
