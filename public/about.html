About NotifAi News

NotifAi News summarizes and debates the world’s biggest stories using AI. Our aim is quick clarity, visible viewpoints, and respectful disagreement.

Stories refresh hourly (moving toward every 30 seconds), with more vetted sources rolling out soon. We’re also building native apps for Android & iOS and evolving single-takes into full conversational debates.

We plan a community-driven NotifAi Token to fund scaling, apps, and custom LLMs optimized for summarization and viewpoint diversity.
