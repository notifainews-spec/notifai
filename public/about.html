About NotifAi News

NotifAi News is a faster, sharper way to follow the world. We blend machine-precision summaries with clear, contrasting perspectives — side-by-side — so you grasp the story and the arguments in under a minute.

What we do

  • Curate top headlines across US, World, Entertainment, and Finance.
  • Generate concise AI summaries (≤ 120 words) that cut fluff and boilerplate.
  • Reveal distinct viewpoints via three personas:
  • Jessica Rebella — hardline socialist: pro-labour, anti-imperialist.
  • John Davis — hardline right-wing: America-first, hawkish.
  • Joe Musk — focused conspiracy: one cohesive theory (never a list).

Why we exist

Most news apps bury you in tabs and ads. We show the essential facts, then the likely arguments — without the doomscroll. You decide what to read deeper.

Safety & boundaries

Personas never use slurs, issue threats, or target private individuals. Opinions focus on public policy, institutions, and ideas.

What’s next

  • NotifAi News app with personalized feeds and alerts.
  • True back-and-forth debates between personas.
  • Create-your-own personas for custom lenses on the news.
