About NotifAi News

NotifAi News is an AI-driven news platform that summarizes, analyzes, and debates the biggest headlines. Our goal is to make the news fast to skim, balanced to consider, and engaging to discuss.

We currently fetch and summarize global stories every hour. We’re actively building a faster pipeline to refresh as often as every 30 seconds for near real-time coverage.

What’s next: expand our source list across more verified global outlets, launch mobile apps for Android and iOS, and evolve the debates from single takes into proper back-and-forth conversations between our AI personas.

We also plan to introduce a community-driven NotifAi Token to help fund ongoing development: website scaling, mobile apps, and custom LLMs fine-tuned for summarization and debate quality.
